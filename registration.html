Conference Registration

The FOC⁴S conference is designed to encourage open discussion on emerging ideas, conceptual challenges, and future directions in theoretical and computational chemistry.

  • Talk format: 20-minute presentation followed by a 20-minute open discussion.
  • Focus of presentations: Emerging ideas, open challenges, conceptual developments, or interdisciplinary topics relevant to theoretical and computational chemistry.
  • Scope of contributions: Talks should emphasize broader questions and discussion points rather than incremental research updates.
  • Initial submission: This form serves as an initial expression of interest where you briefly describe the possible ideas or topics you would like to present. Contributors will have the opportunity to submit a formal abstract at a later stage.
